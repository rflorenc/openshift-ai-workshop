    Lab 4: RAG with Milvus, Docling and Llama Stack (60 min)¶

    Lab · 60 min

    You ground a language model's answers in documents (RAG, retrieval-augmented generation): first in the OpenShift AI Playground, then step by step in code. You turn text into embeddings, store and search them in the vector database Milvus, convert a complex PDF with Docling, and finally let Llama Stack do chunking, embedding, storing and retrieving behind one API.
